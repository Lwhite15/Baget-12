# Baget support

Email **bagetsupport@gmail.com**. We usually reply within a day or two.

In the app, you can also go to **Profile → Send feedback**, or shake your phone to report a problem with a screenshot.

## Common questions

**My scouts haven't found anything yet.**
A new scout runs its first hunt within a few minutes. On Free, scouts hunt once a day during your 4-hour hunting window, which you can change in Settings. On Pro, they hunt every 6 hours, around the clock.

**How do I cancel Baget Pro?**
Go to Profile → Manage subscription, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

**I paid but don't have Pro.**
Go to Profile → Go Pro → Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, email us with roughly when you bought it.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Profile → Delete my account. This removes your account, scouts, finds, photos, chats and feedback from our servers right away.

**Does Baget buy things for me?**
No. Baget finds products and links you to the store. You always check out yourself.
