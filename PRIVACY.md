# Baget Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Baget is a personal shopping scout. This policy explains what Baget collects, why, who can see it, and your choices.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your sign-in (Sign in with Apple or email), display name, username, and your time zone so hunts and notifications arrive at sensible hours.
- **Profile extras (optional):** an icon (an emoji, color or photo you pick) and your birthday (month and day only, no year).
- **Your taste:** the scouts you create, what you tell them, photos you share with them, who you shop for (women's or men's), your sizes and budget, and your likes, passes, watches and purchases logged in the app.
- **Language:** the language you use Baget in, so your scouts write to you in it.
- **Feedback:** if you send feedback, your message and any screenshot you attach.
- **Notifications:** a device token so Baget can send you notifications.
- **Usage:** anonymous app events (screens opened, buttons tapped) tied to a random install ID, not your name or email. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Purchases:** if you subscribe to Baget Pro, Apple (or Google Play on Android) sends us a record of the subscription (product, dates). We never see your payment details.

## How we use it
To find products for you, learn your taste, power friend features, send you notifications, run Baget Pro, and improve the app.

Service providers that process data for us:
- **Anthropic (Claude)** runs your scouts, chat, photo reading and Taste Cards. It receives your scouts' briefs, your chats with them, photos you choose to share, your first name so scouts can address you, your app language, and, for your Taste Card, the brands and styles you like and items you've liked. It doesn't receive your email address.
- **Google (Gemini)** runs a second scout alongside Claude on hunts. It receives your scouts' briefs (what they hunt, the brands and styles you like, and what you liked or passed on) and your app language, and searches the web with Google. It doesn't receive your name or email address.
- **Search and shopping sources (Serper/Google search, Brave Search, eBay)** receive the search terms your scouts use. No personal details are sent.
- **Supabase** hosts the database, storage and server functions.
- **Apple** delivers notifications and handles Baget Pro payments on iPhone. **Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Play)** does the same on Android.

Anthropic doesn't use data sent through its API to train its models.

## Friends
Baget has optional friend features. Here's what friends can see:
- **Anyone you're friends with:** your display name, username, icon (including an icon photo), and your birthday if you've added one.
- **Only when you both have "Share taste with friends" on:** your taste match with them, the brands and styles you like, items you've liked, watched or bought, your Taste Card (a short AI-written summary of your taste), and gift ideas based on your taste.

"Share taste with friends" is on by default. Turn it off in Settings any time and friends see only the basics above. Remove your birthday or icon photo in Settings any time. If you share your Taste Card outside Baget, it goes wherever you send it.

## Sharing
We don't sell your data. Store links may go through an affiliate network so Baget earns a commission when you buy; the store sees a normal visit, including when you open a store page inside Baget. Friends you add see only what's described above.

## Your choices
- Edit or delete anything Baget has learned in "What Baget knows about you".
- Change your username, icon, birthday and "Share taste with friends" in Settings.
- Turn off usage events in Settings, and notifications in your phone's settings.
- Delete your account any time from your profile. This removes your account, scouts, finds, photos (including your icon photo), chats, friendships, Taste Card, birthday and feedback from our servers right away. Anonymous usage events aren't linked to your account, so they stay. Products your scouts found stay in Baget's shared catalog, without any link to you.

## Children
Baget isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. Baget doesn't allow scouts to hunt adult content.

## Changes
If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
