# Baget Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Baget is a personal shopping scout. This policy explains what Baget collects, why, and your choices.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your sign-in (Sign in with Apple or email), display name and handle.
- **Your taste:** the agents you create, what you tell them, photos you share with them, and your likes, passes and purchases logged in the app.
- **Notifications:** a device token so Baget can send you notifications.
- **Usage:** anonymous app events (screens opened, buttons tapped) tied to a random install ID, not your name or email. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Purchases:** if you subscribe to Baget Pro, Apple sends us a signed record of the subscription (product, dates). We never see your payment details.

## How we use it
To find products for you, learn your taste, send you notifications, run Baget Pro, and improve the app.

Service providers that process data for us:
- **Anthropic (Claude)** runs your scouts, chat and photo reading. It receives your agents' briefs, your chats with them, photos you choose to share, and your first name so scouts can address you. It doesn't receive your email address.
- **Serper (Google search)** receives the search terms your scouts use. No personal details are sent.
- **Google (Gemini)** runs a second scout alongside Claude on hunts. It receives your scouts' briefs (what they hunt, the brands and styles you like, and what you liked or passed on) and searches the web with Google. It doesn't receive your name or email address.
- **Supabase** hosts the database, storage and server functions.
- **Apple** delivers notifications and handles Baget Pro payments.

Anthropic doesn't use data sent through its API to train its models.

## Sharing
We don't sell your data. Store links may go through an affiliate network so Baget earns a commission when you buy; the store sees a normal visit. Friends you add see only what your privacy settings allow.

## Your choices
Edit or delete anything Baget has learned in "What Baget knows about you". Turn off usage events in Settings. Delete your account any time from your profile: this removes your account, scouts, finds, photos, chats and feedback from our servers right away. Anonymous usage events aren't linked to your account, so they stay. Products your scouts found stay in Baget's shared catalog, without any link to you.

## Children
Baget isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Contact
Questions or requests about your data: email bagetsupport@gmail.com.
