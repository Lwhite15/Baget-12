# Baget Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Baget is a personal shopping scout. This policy explains what Baget collects, why, and your choices.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your sign-in (Sign in with Apple or email), display name and handle.
- **Your taste:** the agents you create, what you tell them, photos you share with them, and your likes, passes and purchases logged in the app.
- **Notifications:** a device token so Baget can send you notifications.
- **Usage:** anonymous app events (screens opened, buttons tapped) tied to a random install ID, not your name or email. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Purchases:** if you subscribe to Baget Pro, Apple sends us a signed record of the subscription (product, dates). We never see your payment details.

## How we use it
To find products for you, learn your taste, send you notifications, run Baget Pro, and improve the app. Product searches are done by AI (Anthropic's Claude) on our servers using your agents' briefs; your name and contact details aren't sent with them.

## Sharing
We don't sell your data. Store links may go through an affiliate network so Baget earns a commission when you buy; the store sees a normal visit. Friends you add see only what your privacy settings allow.

## Your choices
Edit or delete anything Baget has learned in "What Baget knows about you". Delete your account any time from your profile; this removes your data from our servers.

## Contact
Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Lwhite15/Baget-12 or reach the developer through the App Store listing.
