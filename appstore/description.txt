Whatever you're into, Baget finds it. Rare vinyl, a vintage chair, the perfect gift, a new watch, your next car or the sneakers everyone wants: name it, and your own AI scouts hunt the web for it.

Tell Baget what you're into, tap a few brands and vibes, and your scouts get to work. They search official stores, big retailers, resale sites and small boutiques, check the price and stock, and text you when they find something worth your time.

YOUR SCOUTS
• Each scout hunts one thing you care about: mid-century lamps under $300, a first-press record, a gift for your dad, Jordans in your size
• They find drops, restocks and deals, plus picks you'd never have searched for
• Every find comes with a real photo, the price, the store and why it fits your taste
• Chat with a scout to steer it: "more like this", "nothing over $200", "skip that brand"

LEARNS YOUR TASTE
• A quick taste check at setup, then every like and pass sharpens what your scouts look for
• No need to be an expert: each find says who the brand is and what the product is. For fragrances, it tells you what it smells like and when to wear it
• Your For You feed on the Today screen always has something new, chosen for you

TEXTS THAT FEEL PERSONAL
• Your scouts write to you like a friend with great taste: what they found, the key fact and why it's for you
• Choose how often they text, turn on overnight quiet hours, or keep a scout quiet

BUYING STAYS IN YOUR HANDS
• Baget links you straight to the store's product page. You always check out yourself
• See how a price compares with other stores before you buy

SHOP WITH FRIENDS
• Add friends, share finds and get their opinion

BAGET PRO
Free includes 2 scouts that hunt once a day during a 4-hour window you choose.
Baget Pro gives you up to 5 scouts that hunt around the clock, every 6 hours, with twice as many searches and more stores on every hunt.

Baget Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings.
